Phase 1

Initial Shock In The Northeast

The first wave concentrated around high-density blocks near the corridor edge. Most nearby streets remained functional but heavily congested.

Key signal: sudden drop in service access within 48 hours.
Phase 2

Pressure Moves Inward

By month two, the central district became the primary hotspot. Satellite traces show contiguous structural damage rather than isolated impact points.

Key signal: contiguous damage area more than doubled.
Phase 3

Repeated Clearing Cycles

Built-up zones in the east underwent repeated clearance. Residents reported multiple return-attempts followed by renewed displacement.

Key signal: repeated movement in and out of the same 2 km zone.
Phase 4

Southern Belt Consolidates

In the final period, the southern belt shifted fastest. What had been mixed-use streets now appears as wide cleared strips intersecting residential pockets.

Key signal: block-level continuity in cleared pathways.
