Lineage and continuity

The community

The bell-metal tradition of Mannar lives through the families and artisans who have carried its knowledge across generations. Their work is shaped not only by inherited techniques, but by a deep understanding of metal, fire, sound, and the place they call home.

For these makers, craft is more than an occupation. It is a source of livelihood, identity, and community — connecting generations through shared skills, workshops, stories, and everyday practices
